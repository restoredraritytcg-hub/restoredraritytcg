Card Check

Take a photo or upload a scan. You get the card straightened, its centering measured, close-ups of all four corners and edges, the best grade the centering allows at PSA, CGC and Beckett, and photos ready for an eBay listing. Everything runs on your device. Your pictures aren't uploaded anywhere.

Or drop photos/scans onto a Front or Back box.
  • Lay the card flat on a plain, dark surface that's a different color from the card's border.
  • Hold the phone directly above the card, close enough that the card fills most of the picture.
  • Take it out of the sleeve or toploader, and avoid glare from overhead lights.
  • Scanner files work too. A scan with up to 3 cards fills this card and the next ones.
