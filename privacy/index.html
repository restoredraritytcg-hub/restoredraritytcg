Privacy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. This page is a draft and should be reviewed by counsel (Leroy) before it is treated as final. Items marked [to confirm] still need a business-specific answer.

This notice describes how Restored Rarity TCG uses information collected through restoredraritytcg.com. Restored Rarity is Scott’s card cleaning, repair and grading-prep service, based in Conroe, Texas, and serving greater Houston and mail-in customers. The legal business name to list here is Restored Rarity TCG.

Who we are

Questions: restoredraritytcg@gmail.com or (281) 814-1968.

What we collect

The public site may collect:

  • From the submission form: your name, email, phone number, how you will get cards to us, a return address when needed, a referral code, card details, declared values, the services you want, notes, photos of the cards, and your agreement to the terms on the form
  • From the Track tool: your reference number and email, so we can show the status of a submission
  • From the review form: your name, rating and comments
  • If you use the customer portal with a link we send you, the order and photo information tied to that submission

The Card Check tool on this site processes photos in your browser and is not set up to upload them.

How that information is handled

  • Submissions are sent to a Google Apps Script endpoint and a Google Sheet, and used to email you about your order from restoredraritytcg@gmail.com.
  • A copy of the submission is also saved in a Supabase database used by the owner portal (customer profiles, cards, notes, statuses and photos).
  • Photos from the submission form are uploaded to Cloudinary, and may also be stored in the portal’s file storage.
  • The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.
  • This site does not currently include a visitor-analytics or advertising pixel. If that changes, this page should be updated.
