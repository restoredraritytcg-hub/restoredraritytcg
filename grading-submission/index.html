How it works

My grading submission fee ($15.00 for the first card and $5.00 for each one after on its own, or $5.00 per card added to another service) covers evaluating, packaging and sending your card to the grading company you choose: PSA, CGC, BGS or TAG.

The grading company's own fees are separate. Grader fees are set by each grader and are subject to change. Current PSA service levels are listed on the pricing section of the home page (checked November 2026 and editable if they change).

Grading orders are paid in four steps:

  1. Up front: my grading submission fee, plus the Grade-Ready Evaluation fee if you chose one.
  2. When your cards ship to the grading company: 50% of the grading company's base fee for your service level.
  3. 14 days after they ship: the remaining 50% of the base fee, unless we've agreed on something different.
  4. Final invoice, when grades are back: return shipping and any grading company upcharge (for example, if the grader moves your card to a higher value tier). Due within 72 hours of the invoice.

Tracking

PSA submissions are tracked step by step: you'll see when your card ships to PSA, arrives, is graded and is on its way back, on your private card link and in Track Your Submission.

There's no storefront: hand your cards off in person at a card show, or ship them in from anywhere in the US. Every submission starts online, and shipping details are confirmed by email before you send anything. Restored Rarity is based in Conroe and serves collectors across greater Houston.
